Import from ESPN Fantasy

Bring your fantasy team's roster into My Players. Works for public leagues; nothing is sent anywhere but ESPN.

Find it on ESPN: open your league and copy the address bar, or the number after leagueId=. The sport is read from the link.
Private league, or ESPN blocked? Paste roster instead
  1. In a browser where you're signed in to ESPN, open this link and copy everything on the page:
  2. Paste it below and press Use pasted roster, then pick your team. You can also paste player names, one per line.
Limits: ESPN only shares public leagues with outside pages. Private leagues need your ESPN login cookies (espn_s2/SWID), which this dashboard never asks for or stores. Use the paste option instead, or ask your league manager to set League settings → "Make league viewable to public". D/ST (team defense) is imported as a special card with team defensive stats. NBA fantasy isn't supported because NBA isn't one of the dashboard's fantasy sports.
